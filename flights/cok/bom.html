About this route

The route from Kochi International Airport to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport covers 1,064 kilometres and typically takes around one hour and forty minutes. It is served by several carriers, including Air India, IndiGo and SpiceJet, with nonstop service available. The flight can be booked in economy, premium economy or business class. Demand for this corridor is moderate, with a peak in October, November, December and May. The departure terminal at Kochi is Terminal 1, while arrivals at Mumbai are handled by Terminals 1 and 2. The journey connects two major Indian cities, offering a convenient link between the southern coast and the financial capital.

Before you book

When you travel from Kochi to Mumbai, choose a departure from Terminal 1 at Cochin International Airport, as all airlines operating on this route use that terminal and it keeps check‑in and security streamlined. Arriving at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, be aware that both Terminals 1 and 2 receive flights; the airline’s assigned arrival terminal is listed in the schedule, so plan your exit route accordingly. Because the flight distance is about 1,064 km and the typical flight time is just under two hours, you can bring a lightweight carry‑on that fits easily in the overhead bin, which helps you avoid delays at the gate. Finally, if you prefer a quieter journey, book a seat in the premium economy section on one of the nonstop flights, as this class offers more legroom and a quieter cabin environment compared to standard economy.
