About this route

The route from Lucknow to Mumbai covers 1,184 kilometres and typically takes about two hours and five minutes. It is served by several carriers, including IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, and Akasa Air, with nonstop flights available. The flight schedule offers thirteen departures per day, with IndiGo operating the largest share. Travelers can choose from economy, premium economy, and business cabins on these services. Peak travel months for this route include October, November, December, and May. The departure airport in Lucknow has three terminals, while the arrival airport in Mumbai has two terminals.

Before you book

When flying from Lucknow to Mumbai, choose a departure from terminal 3 at Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport, as all scheduled services use that gate, which helps avoid last‑minute terminal changes. Since the flight duration is roughly two hours and a half, book a seat in the front row of the economy cabin if you want extra legroom for the short journey; the aircraft used by IndiGo and Akasa Air typically offer a slightly more generous pitch than the older Air India Express models. Plan to arrive at the airport at least 90 minutes before departure. The terminal at Lucknow is large, and the security lines can be long, especially during the peak months of October through December and May. Finally, keep an eye on the flight’s arrival terminal in Mumbai. Most carriers arrive at Terminal 2, but some, such as the 6E5148 flight, use Terminal 1, so check the airline’s information before heading to the airport to avoid any confusion when you land.
