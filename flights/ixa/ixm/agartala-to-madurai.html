Travelling from Agartala to Madurai: what you need to know

  • Flights depart from Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA) in Agartala.
  • Arrivals are at Madurai Airport (IXM) in Madurai.
  • There are currently no non-stop flights on this route; most journeys involve one or more stops.
  • The timezone is the same for both cities (IST, UTC+5:30).
  • The busiest airport-to-airport pairing for this route typically involves a connection in Kolkata or Chennai.
  • Passengers are advised to arrive at the airport approximately 2 hours before domestic departures, especially during peak festival seasons.

How to find the cheapest flights from Agartala to Madurai

  • Booking 3 to 6 weeks in advance generally yields the most competitive fares for this domestic route.
  • September and October are often among the cheaper months to fly before the winter peak.
  • Setting up price alerts for IXA to IXM allows travellers to track fluctuations in fare prices.
  • Use search filters to toggle between the cheapest options and the fastest routes to avoid excessively long layovers.

Flying from Agartala: Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport

  • Major airlines operating from IXA include IndiGo and Air India.
  • Common connection hubs for flights to Madurai include Kolkata (CCU) and occasionally Delhi (DEL).
  • The airport is located approximately 15 km from Agartala city centre.
  • Travel time to the airport by car or taxi is roughly 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.
  • Public transport options include auto-rickshaws and local taxis.

Arriving in Madurai: Madurai Airport

  • Madurai Airport (IXM) is situated approximately 12 km from the city centre.
  • Taxis and prepaid cabs are the primary modes of transport, taking about 20 to 30 minutes to reach the city.
  • Local auto-rickshaws are widely available outside the arrivals terminal for shorter journeys.
  • Once in the city, auto-rickshaws and app-based cabs are the most efficient ways to navigate to the Meenakshi Temple area.

Best time to go to Madurai

  • The most popular time to visit is between October and March when the weather is cooler.
  • June to August is generally quieter and cheaper, though it can be very hot.
  • Summer temperatures typically range from 25°C to 40°C.
  • Winter temperatures are more moderate, usually ranging from 20°C to 30°C.

More about Madurai

  • The Meenakshi Amman Temple is the city's most iconic landmark and a masterpiece of Dravidian architecture.
  • Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal is a 17th-century palace known for its massive pillars and ornate ceilings.
  • The Alagar Koyil temple, located on a scenic hill, is a significant pilgrimage site outside the main city.

Explore more of Tamil Nadu

  • Chennai: Approximately 460 km from Madurai, known for Marina Beach and the Kapaleeshwarar Temple.
  • Coimbatore: Approximately 220 km from Madurai, famous for its textile industry and proximity to the Nilgiri hills.
  • Ooty: Approximately 290 km from Madurai, a hill station renowned for its botanical gardens and tea plantations.

Finding cheap flights from Agartala to Madurai: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Agartala to Madurai?

To find cheap flights from Agartala (IXA) to Madurai (IXM), you should monitor fares on this page and book at least four to six weeks in advance. Prices are generally lower during the monsoon season from June to September. Flexibility with travel dates is essential, as mid-week departures often cost less than weekend flights. Checking nearby hubs like Kolkata or Chennai for connecting flights can also reveal lower fares for this long-distance domestic route.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Agartala to Madurai?

The cheapest way to fly from Agartala to Madurai is typically by booking a flight with a layover in a major hub such as Kolkata (CCU) or Chennai (MAA). Low-cost carriers like IndiGo often provide the most competitive rates on this route. To secure the lowest price, use the price alert tools on this page to track fare drops and avoid booking during peak festival seasons like Diwali or Pongal.

When is the best time to book a flight from Agartala to Madurai to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from Agartala to Madurai is typically between 30 and 60 days before departure. Booking during this window allows you to avoid the price hikes associated with last-minute travel. Because this route involves significant distance and usually requires connections, securing a seat early ensures better availability on the most efficient flight paths. Monitor the live price trends on this page for the most current data.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Agartala to Madurai?

The cheapest months to fly from Agartala to Madurai are generally during the rainy season, specifically July, August, and September. During these months, demand for travel to Tamil Nadu and Tripura decreases, leading airlines to offer lower fares. Conversely, prices peak during the winter months of December and January when weather is more favourable for tourism and family visits across India.

How long is the flight from Agartala to Madurai?

The total travel time from Agartala (IXA) to Madurai (IXM) typically ranges from 6 to 10 hours depending on the length of the layover. There are no direct flights on this route, so the journey includes a flight to a hub like Kolkata or Chennai followed by a connecting flight to Madurai. The actual time spent in the air is approximately 4 to 5 hours.

Which airlines fly from Agartala to Madurai?

IndiGo is the primary airline operating flights from Agartala to Madurai, typically via connections in Kolkata or Chennai. Air India may also serve this route through its network of hubs. These carriers provide the necessary connectivity between Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport and Madurai Airport, though passengers will always have at least one stop due to the distance and regional flight patterns.

Are there direct flights from Agartala to Madurai?

There are currently no direct non-stop flights from Agartala (IXA) to Madurai (IXM). All passengers must take a connecting flight, most commonly through Kolkata (CCU) or Chennai (MAA). The distance of 2093 kilometres and the specific regional demand make connecting services the only viable option for this route.

How much does a flight from Agartala to Madurai usually cost?

Flight prices from Agartala to Madurai typically range between 8,000 and 15,000 INR for a round trip, depending on the season. Fares are driven by demand during major Indian festivals and the cost of connecting flights through hubs. Booking during the off-peak monsoon season generally results in fares at the lower end of this range, while peak winter travel increases costs.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly from Agartala to Madurai for a lower fare?

Flying from Agartala to Madurai on Tuesdays or Wednesdays typically offers the lowest fares. Mid-week flights avoid the business travel rush seen on Mondays and the leisure travel peaks on Fridays and Sundays. Additionally, choosing flights with very early morning departures from Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport can sometimes result in lower costs and better connection timings at the hub airport.

Which airports serve Agartala and Madurai, and how far are they from the city centre?

Agartala is served by Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA), located approximately 15 kilometres from the city centre. Madurai is served by Madurai Airport (IXM), which is situated about 10 kilometres from the city centre. Both airports feature a single terminal building and are accessible via taxis and auto-rickshaws, which are the primary modes of ground transport in both cities.

How many flights operate between Agartala and Madurai, and what is the distance?

There are several connecting flight options daily between Agartala and Madurai, though no single direct service exists. The total flight distance between Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA) and Madurai Airport (IXM) is 2093 kilometres. Most itineraries involve one stop, with the number of daily options depending on the connection schedules of carriers like IndiGo in Kolkata or Chennai.

What is the best time of year to visit Madurai, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Madurai is from November to February when the weather is cool and pleasant. During these winter months, temperatures are mild, making it ideal for visiting the Meenakshi Amman Temple. From March to May, Madurai is very hot, and from June to October, the city experiences significant rainfall during the southwest and northeast monsoons.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade-off?

IndiGo is generally the cheapest airline for travel from Agartala to Madurai. The trade-off for the lower fare is that IndiGo is a low-cost carrier, meaning that amenities such as in-flight meals and checked baggage may incur additional fees. Passengers seeking a full-service experience with included meals may find Air India more suitable, though typically at a higher price point.

What is the most common connecting hub for flights from Agartala to Madurai?

Kolkata (CCU) is the most common connecting hub for flights from Agartala to Madurai due to its proximity to Tripura. Most passengers fly from Agartala to Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport in Kolkata before boarding a second flight to Madurai. Chennai (MAA) serves as the second most frequent hub, particularly for those using South India-focused flight paths.
