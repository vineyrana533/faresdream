About this route

The route from Bengaluru to Pune covers a distance of 723 kilometres and typically takes about 90 minutes of flight time. Flights are operated nonstop, allowing for a direct journey between the two cities. Several carriers serve this corridor, including Air India, IndiGo and SpiceJet, each offering a range of cabin classes from Economy to Business. Bengaluru’s Kempegowda International Airport, a major hub with two terminals, is the departure point, while Pune Airport, a single‑terminal facility, receives the arrivals. Demand for this route is moderate, with a demand index of 46, and travel peaks during the months of October, November, December and May.

Before you book

Choose a flight that departs in the early morning or late evening to avoid the busier airport crowds at Kempegowda International Airport. If you prefer a more relaxed cabin, book a seat in premium economy or business class on any of the airlines that serve this route, as the 90‑minute flight offers a quick escape from the city. Plan to arrive at the airport at least 90 minutes before departure, allowing time for the two‑terminal layout and the possibility of a brief walk between terminals if you need to change. Since Pune Airport has only one terminal, you can use the arrival time to explore nearby cafés or the city’s historic sites, making the short flight a convenient gateway to a day of sightseeing.
