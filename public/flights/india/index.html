How to find cheap flights from India (IN)

Looking for a cheap ticket from India? Here are a few tips to help you score the best fares and make your journey as smooth as possible.

  • Enter your destination and hit ‘Search’. We compare FaresDream fares from airports across India against major airlines and travel agents, so what you see is what you pay — no hidden fees.
  • Fly from a nearby airport. India has dozens of airports; widening your search to include a second airport near you often reveals a cheaper fare.
  • Travel in the low season. Fares to and from India swing with the seasons — flying outside the summer and festival peaks is usually the cheapest way to go.
  • Be flexible with your dates. Mid-week departures are often cheaper than weekends; shifting your trip by a day or two can lower the fare noticeably.
  • Compare cabins. Economy is the cheapest, but on longer routes from India a premium-economy fare booked early can be closer to economy than you'd expect.
  • Book at the right time. For most routes from India, booking a few weeks to a couple of months ahead tends to land the best fare.
  • Look for direct flights. Non-stop services from India's major hubs save hours; filter for direct flights when the small saving of a connection isn't worth the time.
