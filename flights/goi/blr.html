About this route

The route from Goa’s Dabolim Airport to Bengaluru’s Kempegowda International Airport covers a distance of 483 kilometres and typically takes around 72 minutes in nonstop flight. Air India, IndiGo, and SpiceJet operate direct services, with IndiGo offering the highest frequency of flights. The flight can be booked in economy, premium economy, or business class. Demand for this corridor is moderate, with a demand index of 44, and peaks during October, November, December, and May. Bengaluru’s airport, a hub, features two terminals, while Goa’s airport has a single terminal. The journey is served by a mix of carriers, including Air India Express and Alliance Air, alongside the dominant IndiGo presence.

Before you book

Choose a flight that lands in Terminal 1 at Kempegowda; the terminal offers a smoother transfer to the city’s metro and bus network, making the final leg to your hotel quicker. Book a seat on a morning departure from Goa. Early‑morning flights give you a full day in Bengaluru, and the airport’s check‑in counters open early, reducing the time spent waiting. If you prefer a relaxed journey, opt for a flight that arrives before 12 p.m. This allows you to avoid the late‑afternoon traffic congestion that often surrounds the airport during peak hours. Since Goa and Bengaluru share the same time zone, you can plan your itinerary without worrying about jet lag or time‑zone confusion, which is handy for coordinating meetings or sightseeing that start early in the day.
