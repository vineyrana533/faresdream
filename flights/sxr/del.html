About this route

The route from Srinagar’s Sheikh ul‑Alam International Airport to Delhi’s Indira Gandhi International Airport covers 643 kilometres and typically takes about 84 minutes. It is served by non‑stop flights and is available in economy, premium economy, and business cabins. Air India, IndiGo, and SpiceJet all operate on this corridor, with Air India belonging to the Star Alliance. Demand for the route is moderate, with a demand index of 33, and it peaks during October, November, December, and May. The two airports are both in India, share the same time zone of Asia/Kolkata, and the Delhi hub offers three terminals for passenger handling.

Before you book

When traveling from Srinagar to Delhi, consider arriving at Sheikh ul‑Alam International Airport early in the morning to avoid the peak traffic that builds up as the day progresses. The flight is short—just over an hour—so a light, easily storable carry‑on will keep you comfortable and avoid congestion at the airport. Since the route is served by several airlines, compare the in‑flight meal options and entertainment availability to choose the one that best matches your preferences. Finally, plan your transfer from Indira Gandhi International Airport in Delhi by selecting a reliable local transport service that can navigate the busy city traffic, especially during rush hours.
