About this route

Siliguri’s Bagdogra Airport (IXB) serves as a regional gateway, while Bengaluru’s Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) functions as a major hub in southern India. The flight covers approximately 1,863 kilometres and typically takes around 177 minutes, with nonstop service available. Air India, IndiGo, and SpiceJet operate on this route, offering economy, premium economy, and business cabin options. Demand for the connection is moderate, with peak travel periods in October, November, December, and May. Both airports share the same time zone, simplifying time‑zone considerations for travelers.

Before you book

If you’re traveling from Siliguri to Bengaluru, book your flight well in advance to secure the best seat placement, especially on the popular October through May period when demand spikes. Arriving at Bagdogra Airport early gives you ample time to navigate the single terminal and complete any security checks without rushing. During the flight, bring a lightweight travel pillow and a small water bottle—airlines often provide water but having your own keeps you comfortable on the nearly three‑hour journey. Finally, keep a digital copy of your boarding pass and any travel documents on your phone; this eliminates the need to carry extra paper and speeds up your exit from the airport upon arrival in Bengaluru.
