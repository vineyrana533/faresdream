About this route

The Chennai to Mumbai route covers a distance of 1,033 kilometres and typically takes just under two hours. Flights on this corridor are nonstop, with a variety of carriers offering regular service, including Air India, IndiGo and Akasa Air. IndiGo operates the majority of daily departures, while Air India provides a solid alternative and Akasa Air offers a single daily flight. The route is served from Chennai International Airport terminals 1 and 4 to Mumbai’s Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport terminals 1 and 2. Demand for this connection is high, with peaks in October, November, December and May. Travelers can choose from economy, premium economy and business cabins on any of the airlines that serve this route.

Before you book

Choose a flight that departs early in the morning or late in the evening to avoid the busiest airport periods and enjoy a quieter boarding experience. Use the airport’s dedicated lounges or the airline’s premium cabins if you want extra comfort on a 1‑hour‑45‑minute journey; the short flight makes a few extra minutes of relaxation worthwhile. Plan your arrival at Chennai International Airport at least 90 minutes before departure, especially when using Terminal 1, to allow time for security checks and to navigate between terminals if you need to change. When you land in Mumbai, take advantage of the airport’s free Wi‑Fi and charging stations in Terminal 2 to catch up on work or entertainment before heading to your next destination.
