Finding cheap flights from India

Looking for a cheap ticket out of India? Here is what you need to know about the airports you can fly from, the airlines that serve them and the best time to book.

Which airports can you fly from in India?

India's international gateways are led by Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR), with Chennai (MAA), Hyderabad (HYD) and Kolkata (CCU) close behind. Regional hubs such as Kochi (COK), Goa (GOI), Ahmedabad (AMD), Pune (PNQ) and Jaipur (JAI) add dozens more departure points, so most travellers have a major airport within easy reach.

Which airlines fly from India?

Air India, IndiGo and Vistara operate the widest domestic and international networks from India. On long-haul routes you will also find Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad Airways, Singapore Airlines, British Airways and Lufthansa. Comparing carriers side by side is the quickest way to see who is cheapest for your dates.

When is the cheapest time to fly from India?

Fares from India are lowest outside the summer holidays and the Diwali and Christmas peaks. The monsoon months, roughly June to September, and the weeks after New Year usually carry the softest prices. Mid-week departures are typically cheaper than weekends, and booking a few weeks to a couple of months ahead lands the best fares on most routes.

How to get the best fare from India

Stay flexible on dates and airports, compare a nearby second airport, and set a price alert so you are told when a fare drops. On long international routes a premium-economy seat booked early can be surprisingly close to economy, and filtering for direct flights saves hours where the small saving of a connection is not worth the time.

Cheap flights from India: frequently asked questions

Which airlines fly from India?

Air India, IndiGo and Vistara run the largest networks from India, alongside international carriers such as Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad Airways, Singapore Airlines, British Airways and Lufthansa. The cheapest carrier varies by route and season, so compare a few options for your exact dates.

What is the cheapest month to fly from India?

Flights from India are generally cheapest outside the summer and festival peaks — the monsoon months from June to September and the weeks after New Year tend to have the softest fares. Prices climb around Diwali, Christmas and the summer school holidays, so travelling either side of those windows saves the most.

How far ahead should I book a flight from India?

For most routes from India, booking a few weeks to about two months ahead lands the best fare. Long-haul international trips reward booking earlier, while last-minute domestic deals do occasionally appear. Setting a price alert lets you watch a route and book when the fare dips.

Which is the busiest airport in India?

Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) is India's busiest, followed by Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) and Bengaluru's Kempegowda International Airport (BLR). These three hubs offer the widest choice of domestic and international flights and usually the most competitive fares.

Are there direct flights from India to major cities abroad?

Yes. Non-stop services connect India's main hubs with the Gulf (Dubai, Doha, Abu Dhabi), Southeast Asia (Singapore, Bangkok), Europe (London, Frankfurt, Paris) and North America (New York, Toronto). Direct options are widest from Delhi and Mumbai; from smaller cities you may need a one-stop connection.

How can I find the cheapest flights from India?

Compare airlines and dates in one place, stay flexible by a day or two, and consider a nearby second airport. Flying mid-week and outside the festival and summer peaks lowers the fare, and a price alert tells you when a route drops so you can book at the right moment.
