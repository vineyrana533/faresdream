Which airports can I depart from in India?

India’s major departure points include Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) in Delhi, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) in Mumbai, Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) in Bengaluru, Chennai International Airport (MAA), Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) in Hyderabad, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU) in Kolkata, and Lucknow International Airport (LKO). Other important hubs are Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD) in Ahmedabad, Pune International Airport (PNQ), and Jaipur International Airport (JAI). These airports offer a mix of domestic and international services and are the primary points of entry for travellers leaving India.

Each of these airports serves a range of airlines, from national carriers to international flagships. They are equipped with modern facilities, multiple terminals, and are connected to major cities by rail, metro, and road links, making them convenient for both business and leisure travellers.

Which airlines operate from India?

Domestic services are dominated by Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, Go First, Vistara, and AirAsia India. These carriers provide extensive networks across the country and offer competitive fares, especially on short‑haul routes.

Internationally, Indian airports are served by Emirates, Qatar Airways, Turkish Airlines, Singapore Airlines, Lufthansa, Air France, British Airways, and Etihad. Low‑cost carriers such as AirAsia X and Scoot also operate long‑haul flights to Southeast Asia and beyond. Many foreign airlines operate codeshare agreements with Indian carriers, expanding connectivity.

When is the cheapest time to fly from India?

The low‑season for Indian departures generally falls between January and March, and again from September to November. During these months, demand is lower, which tends to drive fares down.

Avoid the peak periods of June to August, mid‑December, and the New Year holiday window, when tourism and business travel surge. Even mid‑December can be cheaper than Christmas if you book early, but prices typically rise as the holiday approaches.

What is the best booking window to secure a cheap fare?

For domestic routes, the sweet spot is usually three to five weeks before departure. Booking earlier than this can sometimes lock in lower fares, but last‑minute deals are rare.

International flights tend to be cheapest when booked two to three months in advance. Airlines often release fare classes in early summer or early winter, so monitoring these windows can yield savings.

How can I find the cheapest flights from India?

Start with flight comparison portals that aggregate prices from multiple airlines and travel agents. Set up price alerts for your preferred routes and dates to receive notifications when fares drop.

Check the airlines’ own websites; they sometimes offer exclusive promotions not listed on third‑party sites. Signing up for newsletters from carriers such as IndiGo, Air India, and Emirates can give early access to sales.

What practical tips can reduce costs?

Pack light to avoid checked‑baggage fees on low‑cost carriers. Use public transport or shared rides to reach the airport to save on taxi or ride‑share fares.

Book round‑trip tickets and consider mid‑week departures, as weekends usually command higher prices. If you have flexibility, compare fares from nearby airports; for example, flying out of Pune instead of Mumbai can sometimes offer better deals for certain destinations.

Cheap flights from India: frequently asked questions

Which airlines fly from India?

India’s airports are served by a mix of domestic and international carriers. Major domestic airlines include Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, Go First, Vistara, and AirAsia India. Internationally, airlines such as Emirates, Qatar Airways, Turkish Airlines, Singapore Airlines, Lufthansa, Air France, British Airways, and Etihad operate flights from Indian hubs. Low‑cost long‑haul carriers like AirAsia X and Scoot also serve key routes to Southeast Asia and beyond.

What is the cheapest month to fly from India?

The most affordable periods are typically January to March and September to November. During these months, demand for flights is lower, which tends to bring fares down. Avoiding the June to August summer months, mid‑December, and the New Year holiday window generally yields cheaper tickets.

How far ahead should I book a flight from India?

For domestic travel, booking three to five weeks in advance usually offers the best prices. International flights are often cheapest when booked two to three months ahead. Monitoring fare releases in early summer or early winter can also uncover early‑bird discounts.

Which is the busiest airport in India?

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) in Delhi is the busiest airport in India, handling the highest passenger traffic and offering the widest range of domestic and international flights.

Are there direct flights from India to major cities abroad?

Yes, many Indian airports operate direct services to major global cities. For example, Delhi and Mumbai have nonstop flights to London, New York, Dubai, Singapore, and Hong Kong. Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad also offer direct routes to key destinations in the Middle East, Europe, and Southeast Asia.

How can I find the cheapest flights from India?

Use flight comparison sites to compare prices across airlines and travel agents. Set price alerts for your desired routes and dates. Check airlines’ own websites for exclusive promotions, and sign up for newsletters to receive early‑bird offers. Flexible travel dates and mid‑week departures often yield lower fares.

What are the best low‑cost carriers for international travel from India?

Low‑cost long‑haul carriers such as AirAsia X and Scoot provide affordable flights from Indian airports to destinations in Southeast Asia, Australia, and the Middle East. For budget options to Europe, airlines like WOW Air (now merged with Icelandair) and some seasonal routes from IndiGo and SpiceJet offer competitive fares.

Can I get cheaper fares by flying from a nearby airport?

Yes, departing from an alternative airport can sometimes reduce costs. For instance, flying out of Pune instead of Mumbai, or Lucknow instead of Delhi, may offer cheaper fares for certain routes, especially if the alternative airport is served by a low‑cost carrier. It is advisable to compare prices and consider travel time to the airport before deciding.
